# Superstore Sales Performance Analytics

### Future Interns – Data Science & Analytics Internship | Task 1 (2026)

## About the Project

As part of my Data Science and Analytics internship at Future Interns, I worked on a project called **Business Sales Performance Analytics**.

For this project, I used the Superstore Sales dataset to understand how a business performs in terms of sales and profit. My main aim was to explore the data, identify useful patterns and present the findings through interactive dashboards using Power BI.

This project gave me an opportunity to work with real-world sales data and understand how data analysis can help businesses make better decisions.

## Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project were:

- To understand the overall sales and profit performance.
- To find the top 10 products based on sales.
- To study how sales change from month to month.
- To compare sales across different product categories.
- To understand the sales performance of different regions.
- To identify which categories and regions generate more profit.
- To suggest possible ways to improve business performance.

## Dataset Used

**Dataset:** Superstore Sales Dataset

**Source:** [Kaggle – Superstore Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/vivek468/superstore-dataset-final)

The dataset contains sales-related information such as:

- Order ID and Order Date
- Product Name and Category
- Sales and Profit
- Customer Details
- Region and State
- Quantity and Discount

## Tools Used

- **Microsoft Power BI** – To create interactive dashboards and visualize the data.
- **Power Query** – To clean and prepare the dataset.
- **Excel/CSV** – To access and work with the raw dataset.

## Data Cleaning and Preparation

Before creating the dashboards, I imported the dataset into Power BI and checked the data to make sure it was ready for analysis.

Some of the preparation steps I worked on included:

- Checking the columns and data types.
- Correcting the date formats in Order Date and Ship Date.
- Checking for data errors.
- Organizing the data for easier analysis.

This step helped me prepare the dataset for creating meaningful visualizations.

## Dashboard Details

I created two dashboard pages in Power BI to present the sales and profit analysis.

### 1. Sales Performance Dashboard

The first page gives an overview of the Superstore's sales performance.

It includes:

- **Total Orders:** Displays the number of orders.
- **Total Sales:** Shows the overall sales generated.
- **Total Profit:** Shows the profit earned.
- **Sales by Category:** Compares sales across Technology, Furniture and Office Supplies.
- **Sales by Region:** Shows how sales differ across the four regions.
- **Top 10 Products by Sales:** Highlights the products with the highest sales.
- **Monthly Sales Trend:** Shows how sales change throughout the year.
- **Year Slicer:** Helps filter the dashboard based on the selected year.

### 2. Profitability Analysis

The second page focuses on comparing profit across different categories and regions.

It includes:

- **Profit by Category:** Helps identify which product categories generate more profit.
- **Profit by Region:** Compares the profit earned from the East, West, Central and South regions.

## Key Insights

After exploring the dashboards, I observed a few interesting patterns in the data:

1. **Category Performance:** Technology generates the highest profit, while Office Supplies also contributes a good amount. Furniture generates comparatively less profit.

2. **Regional Performance:** The West region has the highest profit, followed by East, South and Central.

3. **Top Products:** The Top 10 Products chart helped identify the products that contribute the most to overall sales.

4. **Monthly Sales:** Sales are not the same throughout the year. There are noticeable increases during September and November.

5. **Sales and Profit:** Comparing sales and profit gives a better understanding of how different categories and regions contribute to business performance.

## Recommendations

Based on the observations, I came up with a few suggestions that could help improve business performance:

- Give more attention to products that generate higher sales.
- Explore ways to improve the profit earned from Furniture products.
- Study the sales strategies used in the better-performing regions.
- Understand why sales increase or decrease during certain months.
- Plan suitable offers and marketing activities during months with lower sales.
- Regularly monitor sales and profit to identify areas that need improvement.
